About Dirt Trail Safaris

Dirt Trail Safaris offers personalized wildlife adventures in Uganda, led by passionate guides George Ssemaganda and Gerald Ariho. Explore the diverse landscapes and encounter Uganda's iconic wildlife through our carefully curated tours. Whether you're interested in gorilla trekking, bird watching, or cultural experiences, we provide unforgettable journeys that support local communities and conservation efforts.
